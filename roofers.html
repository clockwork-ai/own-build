    You can't pick up. The customer rings the next firm.

    A third of micro businesses failed to answer incoming calls, and 69% of callers who reached voicemail hung up without leaving a message (Moneypenny, 2016).

    On a roof, in the wind, or driving between jobs, that's the whole morning. A leak at 2am is the same problem after you've packed the van away. The customer with water coming through the ceiling does not wait until you climb down.

    The other jobs pile up around it. The quote from Thursday's survey sits until Sunday. The customer who was happy with the re-roof never gets asked for a Google review. Last winter's guttering job doesn't hear from you until the next storm.
