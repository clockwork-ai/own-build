    The 9pm leak, step by step.

    Every step is logged. Your rules decide what it can do on its own, and anything unusual comes to you first.

    1. 21:02Customer

      A call comes in

      "Got a leak over the back bedroom. Can someone come and look?" You're up a ladder on another job.

    2. 21:02Agent

      It picks up straight away

      It answers in your business name and says it's your assistant, not a person.

    3. 21:03Agent

      It gets the details

      What's happening, their name and postcode, and a photo by text or WhatsApp.

    4. 21:03Agent

      It makes it safe

      It talks them through turning the water off at the stopcock and keeping clear of any electrics near the water, only if it's safe to.

      Your stopcock is usually under the kitchen sink. Turn it clockwise until it stops. Don't touch any switches or sockets near the water.
    5. 21:04Agent

      It checks the job

      The postcode is in your area. Water coming through a ceiling, so it's marked urgent.

    6. 21:05Calendar

      It checks your diary

      It finds the first free slots: 9am or 1pm tomorrow.

    7. 21:06Agent

      It books the job in

      The customer picks 9am. It goes in your calendar and they get a confirmation.

      You're booked in for 9am tomorrow. Keep clear of the area and put a bucket down if it's safe to.
    8. 21:06You

      It tells you, or the engineer

      A summary with the photo, address and slot, by text or WhatsApp. Prices and anything unusual wait for your OK.

      New urgent job: leak, back bedroom ceiling, BL5. Booked 9am tomorrow. Photo attached.
    9. 08:00Agent

      It follows up

      A reminder on the morning of the job, and one polite nudge if they go quiet.
