    Automation does a task. An agentic workflow does a job.

    A customer messages at 9pm about a leak. Plain automation replies "thanks, we'll be in touch".

    Ours asks for a photo and the postcode, checks you cover the area, marks it urgent, offers a survey slot tomorrow and books it. If they go quiet, it follows up. You wake up to a booked job.

    Six parts. Each one takes a job off you.

    We fit only the parts your business needs, in the tools you already use: your phone, WhatsApp, email, calendar and job software.

    1. The catcher

      Every missed call and message gets a reply straight away. It takes the details and books a call-back.

      Sorry we missed you, we're up on a roof. What's the job and your postcode? We'll ring you back today.

    2. The quote engine

      Quotes drafted from your survey notes and photos, ready for you to check and send the same day.

    3. The chaser

      Polite follow-ups on quiet quotes and unpaid invoices, stopped the moment they reply.

    4. The diary

      Bookings, confirmations and reminders, and easy rebooking when the weather turns.

    5. The review asker

      A review request goes out the day a job is signed off.

      Thanks for having us. If you're happy with the roof, a quick review would mean a lot.

    6. The front desk

      Answers "do you cover...?" and "how much is...?" on your site and WhatsApp, in your words.
