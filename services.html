    4 · Every plan

    Review Accelerator

    Reviews and aftercare. Once a job is marked done, texts or WhatsApps go out in your name.

    Same day
    A thank-you, and a direct Google review link. Every customer gets the link.
    About 3 days
    One gentle nudge, if there's no review yet.
    About 2 weeks
    "Everything still OK with the job?" A problem reply comes straight to you. It's flagged as well as the review link, never instead of it. Google doesn't allow review gating.
    2 and 6 months
    "How's things going?" It keeps you in mind for a referral or a word at the pub.
    12 months
    A check-up or service reminder, so there's a reason for the repeat work to come back.

    When I ran my vans, the job got finished and I was straight onto the next one. The review never got asked for, and the customer never heard from me again. I'd done a great job and nobody knew. Now my system does the bit I never had time for. It thanks the customer, asks for the review, and checks in a few weeks and a few months later to see how things are going. So when their neighbour needs a roofer, or your name comes up at the pub, they remember the one who did a great job and kept in touch.

    You approve the wording before anything goes out. No discount for a star. Sample same-day line: "Thanks for having us. A Google review would mean a lot. Here's the link." 83% of people who were asked to leave a review went on to leave one (BrightLocal, 2026, US survey). That's why the ask exists. It is not a promise of your numbers.
