    Fair questions.

    Am I tied in?

    Trade Website on the monthly option and Trade Growth are 12 months. Automations are a one-off fixed price. Looking after them is monthly, and you can cancel any month.

    Will I keep my phone number?

    Yes. The number on your van, your cards and Google stays exactly the same.

    Do I own it? What if I stop paying?

    Yes, you own it. If you stop the monthly care, you keep what I built. The only fixed terms are the Trade Website monthly option and Trade Growth, which are 12 months.

    Will it sound like a robot?

    It tells customers it's your assistant, not a person, and only says what you've approved. Prices and anything odd come to you first. You try it on your own phone before it goes live.

    What about my customers' details?

    They stay in the tools you already use: your phone, email, calendar and job software. I only use what I need to set things up and fix them. Read how I handle data.

    What if I don't know what I need?

    That's what the free call and the Clockwork Check are for. I'll tell you straight what's worth doing and what isn't.

    Do you only work with trades?

    Trades are where I started, but the automations and agents work the same way for any service business: offices, clinics, salons and more.
