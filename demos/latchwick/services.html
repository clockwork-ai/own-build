What we do

Doors, locks and keys.

Six kinds of job, and most weeks they turn into each other. Somebody rings locked out, the lock comes apart in your hand because it has been stiff for a year, and the job that was an opening becomes a cylinder change. Here is each of them on its own.

The six

One at a time.

Emergency lockouts

Shut out of your own house, keys lost or snapped off in the lock. Opened without damage wherever we can, and a straight word first if we cannot.

Lock changes and upgrades

New cylinders, new mortice locks, new keys. The usual reasons are moving in, losing a set, or a lock that has got stiff enough to worry you.

uPVC door lock and mechanism repairs

The door you have to lift and lean on to lock. Usually the gearbox, the alignment or the cylinder, and all three are a repair rather than a new door.

Lock repairs after a break in

Secure tonight first, then put right properly: the lock, the keeps, the frame. Boarding up where a door or window will not shut.

Window locks

What we do not do

Worth saying out loud.

Car and van keys

Vehicle keys need the coding kit for the make and the model, and that is a different trade with different gear. We will tell you so on the phone rather than turn up and have a go.

Alarms and cameras

We fit locks, not electronics. If you want a bell box, a camera or a smart doorbell wiring in, that is an installer's job. We will happily say which door we would sort out first instead.

New doors and windows

We repair what is there, and a lot of the time that is the right answer. When a door really has had it, you want a door firm with a measure and a fitter, and we will say when we think you have got to that point.

Not sure which of those it is?

Most people are not, and that is fine. Tell us what the door is doing and your postcode and we will work it out when we see it.

If it is a break in, ring the police first

Ring 101 to report it, or 999 if you think somebody is still in the house or nearby. Get out and stay out until they say otherwise. Then ring us and we will make the house secure. The locks can wait twenty minutes.

If you are locked out

  1. Try every other door and the back gate before anything else. Plenty of lockouts end with a back door somebody forgot was unlocked.
  2. Ring whoever else holds a key. A spare half a mile away beats any locksmith in the country.
  3. If a child, an animal or a hob left on is shut in, say that the moment you ring. If a child is at risk, ring 999 first.
  4. Do not put your shoulder to it, and do not let a neighbour do it for you. A forced door costs more than any lock.
  5. Get somewhere warm and dry if you can, and keep your phone with you.

If your keys were stolen, or lost along with anything that shows your address, say so. That turns the job from getting you in to changing the locks, because somebody is holding a working key.
