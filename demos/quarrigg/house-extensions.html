On the job

What happens

  1. Set out, dig the footings and get them inspected before anything goes in.
  2. Drains moved or built over properly, with the right cover and access.
  3. Walls up, cavity trays and insulation in as we go.
  4. Roof structure on, felted and covered so the rain stays out.
  5. Openings formed, steels set, and the shell left ready for the fitters.

Questions

Common questions

Can you work from somebody else's drawings?

Yes, and most people come to us that way. Bring the drawings and the approvals and we will price what is on them. If something on the drawing will not build, we tell you before you sign, not after.

What about the drains?

If a drain runs under the extension it either gets diverted or built over with the right protection and an access point. The inspector looks at it before it is covered. It is one of the main things that moves a price after a dig, which is why we check it on the visit.

Will you do the kitchen and the decorating too?

We do the shell and the making good. Kitchens, electrics, plumbing and decorating come from trades we work with regularly, and we keep them in the right order so nobody is standing on anybody.

Do you take a deposit?

On a real site this is where a builder would set out their payment stages. This is a demo, so there is nothing to agree to and nothing to pay.

Want somebody to come and look?

Tell us what you are after and where you are. We will come out, measure up and put it in writing.

Ring us and we will tell you straight

Tell us what has happened and we will say honestly whether it sounds urgent or whether it can wait for a proper look. We will not promise you a time before we know what we are coming to.

While you wait

  1. Keep everyone away from anything loose or leaning, inside and out.
  2. If it is over a path, a drive or the road, block the way in so nobody walks under it.
  3. Do not prop it yourself and do not pull bricks out to have a look.
  4. If a vehicle hit it, or the wall is near a meter or a service, ring the right people first.
  5. Take a few photos from a safe distance and send them when we ask.

If anyone is hurt, or you can smell gas or see damaged cables, call 999 before you call us.
