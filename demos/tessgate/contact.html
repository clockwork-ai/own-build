    Buying your own tiles?

    Send a photograph or the tile details before you order rather than after, and order about ten per cent over the area, fifteen if there are a lot of cuts. Dye lots vary between batches, so keep the spares.

    Before you send it

    The three things we always ask.

    Does any of it sound hollow?

    Tap along the existing tiles with a knuckle or the handle of a screwdriver. A dull knock is solid. A drum means the bed behind has let go. Tell us roughly how much of it drums, because it is the single thing that decides whether the old tile stays or goes.

    Is there an extractor, and does it work?

    Most black grout is a ventilation problem wearing a tiling costume. If the extractor does not run, or the window never gets opened, say so, because new grout in the same room will go the same way and we would rather tell you that now.

    Are you supplying the tiles?

    Perfectly normal, and better on the quote from the start. Send a photograph or the details before you buy, because the size and the material change how long the job takes and sometimes change what has to go on the wall first.

    Tiling is not an emergency trade, with one exception

    Almost nothing a tiler does cannot wait a fortnight. The exception is a cracked or loose tile in a shower or behind a bath where water is already getting past it, because that water is going somewhere and it is usually into the wall, the floor or the ceiling below.

    Stop using that shower first

    1. Use another shower or a bath for now. Every use puts more water behind the tile.
    2. Run a temporary bead of sealant over the crack or the open joint if you have a tube in the house. It is not a repair, it buys you days.
    3. Look at the ceiling in the room underneath. A stain or a soft patch there changes this from a tiling job into something bigger.
    4. Ring and say which it is, so the right person comes.

    What we will not do

    Print a response time. There is no number on this site for how fast anybody turns up, because any number we put here would be made up. Ring and you will be told honestly when somebody can be there.

    If water is coming through a ceiling, or the floor is soft underfoot, that is past a tiler. Get the water off first, then the damage looked at, then the tiling.
