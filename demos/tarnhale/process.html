Process

Six steps.
Start to keys.

You'll know what happens next at every step, and nothing moves on without your OK. Times are typical, and every project is different. Sample

  1. 01

    First chat

    A free 30 minute call or video chat. You tell us the idea. We tell you honestly whether we're the right fit.

    30 minutes Sample
  2. 02

    Site visit and brief

    We visit, measure up and write a short brief together: what you need, what you'd love, and the budget.

    1 to 2 weeks Sample
  3. 03

    Concept design

    Two or three options as sketches and simple 3D views. We pick one together and refine it.

    3 to 6 weeks Sample
  4. 04

    Planning

    We prepare and submit the application, or confirm the work is permitted development. Councils usually aim to decide householder applications within 8 weeks.

    8 to 12 weeks Sample
  5. 05

    Technical drawings

    Detailed drawings for building regulations and for builders to price against.

    4 to 8 weeks Sample
  6. 06

    Builder and build

    We help you choose a builder, then visit site at key stages until you get the keys.

    Depends on the project Sample

Planning timescales are set by your council and can run longer than the target. Check your council's website for current timescales.
