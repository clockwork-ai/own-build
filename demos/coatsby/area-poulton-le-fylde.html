What we get asked for in Poulton-le-Fylde

Mostly the same two things as everywhere: a room or two decorated before something happens, and an outside that has been left a year longer than it should have been. What varies from town to town is the houses, and in Poulton-le-Fylde it tends to be nineteen thirties and post war semis mostly, which means plenty of hall, stairs and landing jobs and a kitchen somebody is deciding whether to replace or repaint.

None of that changes how the job is done. The walls still get washed, filled, caulked, sanded and dusted off before any paint, and the first coat still depends on what the surface actually is rather than on what is quickest. What it changes is what we are looking for when we come round, and how long the preparation takes compared with the painting.

What a visit looks like

Twenty minutes to half an hour. A light held along each wall to see what the filling will really be, a thumbnail on the woodwork to see what the old paint is doing, a look at any ceiling that has been patched, and a proper look at anything stained or damp. Then it goes in writing, room by room, with what is included and what is not on separate lines.

Decorating is not an emergency trade, and we will not pretend it is

Nobody needs a decorator at two in the morning. What people do need, fairly often, is a room or a flat turned round in a short window: a tenancy ending, a house going on the market, a landlord with a void week, a shop shut for two days. That is the job this is about.

What makes it possible

  1. An empty room, or one that can be emptied. Working round furniture is what turns one day into two.
  2. Plain colours. A tenancy white throughout goes on faster than four shades and a feature wall.
  3. Sensible expectations about the woodwork. Oil based paint on a cold week is slow whatever anybody promises.
  4. Honest photographs sent through first, so we know what the walls are before anybody turns up.

What we will not do

Promise you a time on a web page. We do not publish a response time, because any number we put here would be made up. Ring, tell us the date you need it done by, and you will get a yes or a no rather than a maybe.

If there is water coming through a ceiling or paint bubbling off a damp wall, that is not a decorating job yet. Get the leak and the damp sorted first, or the new paint goes the same way as the old.
