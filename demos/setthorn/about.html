About

A firm that does not exist.

Setthorn Driveways and Patios is invented. There is no yard, no tipper and nobody to ring. It is a demo built by Clockwork AI to show a real driveways and patios firm what a site looks like when everything on it is written properly. The story below is made up, and so is everybody in it.

The made up version

Three of us and a tipper. Two on the tools, one measuring, quoting and arguing politely with grab lorry drivers about timings. The name came off an old field boundary on a map, which felt about right for a firm that spends its life at the line where a garden meets a road.

Most of the work arrives as somebody who has already had a quote that was one line and a total. So the way this made up firm works is built round the opposite of that: the dig depth, the stone, the edges and the drainage written down separately, so you can hold the quote against the next one and ask the same questions of both.

How we would like to be judged

By whether the drive is still flat in five years, and by whether the water ends up where we said it would. Laying blocks is the straightforward part of this trade and plenty of people can do it neatly. Digging deep enough, compacting properly and being honest about where the rain is going is the part that decides whether the neat bit survives.

The other half of it is repair before replace. A sunken patch, a tired green surface or a flag that rocks is a morning and a bag of sand far more often than it is a new drive, and we would rather do the small job and be the firm you ring in ten years.

What we will tell you no about

Retaining walls that are actually holding ground back, where you want somebody who can work out what they are holding. Anything on the public footway that the council has not said we may touch. Laying a smart surface over a drive that has already failed. And a dropped kerb we have not got an answer from the council about, however keen everybody is to get started.

This is not an emergency trade, and we will not pretend it is

Nobody is coming out at midnight to lay blocks. What we will do is get a trip hazard made safe quickly, then book the proper repair in daylight.

Make it safe first

  1. Mark it so people can see it. A cone, a bucket, a chair, a bag of sand, anything that stops somebody walking into it in the dark.
  2. If a flag is rocking, lift it right out rather than leaving it to tip under somebody's foot, and stand it against the wall.
  3. Keep the route to the front door clear, even if that means going round the long way for a few days.
  4. Take a photograph with something in it for scale, a shoe or a tape, before anything is moved.

If it is on the pavement rather than your drive

A lifted or broken footway flag outside your boundary belongs to the council, and it is their job to repair it. Report it to them. Do not pay anybody to mend the public footway.

Making safe is not the repair. A sunken patch is lifted, the layer underneath is put right and the same blocks or flags go back down. That gets booked properly rather than rushed in the dark.
