On the day

What happens

  1. We walk round with you and plan every socket, switch and light.
  2. First fix: floors up and cables in, one area at a time.
  3. Plastering and decorating (we can recommend people, or work with yours).
  4. Second fix: sockets, switches and fittings on, then the new board.
  5. Full testing, a certificate, and a walk round to show you what's where.

Common questions

Rewires: questions

How do I know if I need a rewire?

Common signs are very old cable with rubber or fabric sheathing, no earth on the lighting, round-pin sockets, or a very old board. An inspection is the only way to know for sure.

Can we live in the house while you rewire?

Often yes, especially for a part rewire. For a full rewire it's easier if you can be out for the noisiest days. We'll plan it around you.

How long does a rewire take?

A typical three-bed terrace takes around a week of electrical work ${S}, plus plastering. We'll give you a day-by-day plan.

Will it make a mess?

Some, unavoidably. We sheet everything, lift only what we need to, and clear up every day.

Tell us about the job and send a photo if you can. We'll come back with a fixed quote, no surprises.

Safe first, while you wait

  1. If it's safe to reach, switch off the main switch on your consumer unit (fuse board).
  2. Don't touch damaged sockets, switches, cables or anything that's wet.
  3. Keep everyone, and pets, away from the area.
  4. Call us on the number above and tell us what you can see, hear or smell.

Fire, smoke or someone has had a shock? Call 999 first. Don't touch a person who is still in contact with electricity.
