Emergency help

Stay safe first. Then call.

Simple steps for the most common electrical emergencies. If in doubt, keep away and call an electrician.

999

Fire, smoke, or someone has had an electric shock? Call 999 straight away. Don't touch anyone who's still in contact with electricity: switch off at the board first if you safely can.

Power's gone in one room

  1. Check your consumer unit. Has one switch flipped down?
  2. Unplug things in that room, then push the switch back up once.
  3. If it stays up, plug things back in one by one. The one that trips it is your culprit.
  4. If it trips with nothing plugged in, leave it off and call us.

The fuse board keeps tripping

  1. Don't keep resetting it. It's tripping for a reason.
  2. Leave the tripped switch off.
  3. Note what was on when it went, it helps us find it faster.
  4. Call us. Most faults like this are found quickly Sample.

Burning smell, sparks or scorch marks

  1. If it's safe to reach, switch off the main switch at the consumer unit.
  2. Don't touch the socket, switch or fitting, even if it looks fine now.
  3. Keep everyone away. If there's smoke or flames, get out and call 999.
  4. Call us once everyone's safe.

Water near the electrics

  1. Don't touch anything electrical while you're standing in water.
  2. If you can reach the consumer unit from a dry spot, switch off the main switch.
  3. If you can't reach it safely, leave the room and call us.
  4. Don't switch back on until it's been checked.

General safety information only. Never open or work on anything live yourself.
