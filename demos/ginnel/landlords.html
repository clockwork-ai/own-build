What is an EICR?

An Electrical Installation Condition Report is an inspection of the fixed wiring in a property: the board, the circuits, sockets, switches and fixed fittings. It says whether the installation is safe to keep using, and lists anything that needs attention.

When do you need one?

In England, private landlords need a report at least every five years, or sooner if the last report says so, and tenants should get a copy. Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland have their own rules.

This is a plain-English summary, not legal advice. Check the current government guidance for your property, or ask us and we'll point you to it.

Safe first, while you wait

  1. If it's safe to reach, switch off the main switch on your consumer unit (fuse board).
  2. Don't touch damaged sockets, switches, cables or anything that's wet.
  3. Keep everyone, and pets, away from the area.
  4. Call us on the number above and tell us what you can see, hear or smell.

Fire, smoke or someone has had a shock? Call 999 first. Don't touch a person who is still in contact with electricity.
