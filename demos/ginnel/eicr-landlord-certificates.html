Common questions

EICR electrical certificates: questions

What does an EICR check?

The fixed electrical installation: the consumer unit, wiring, sockets, switches and fixed fittings. It doesn't test appliances like kettles or TVs.

What do the codes mean?

C1 means danger is present, C2 means potentially dangerous, both need fixing. C3 means improvement recommended. FI means further investigation is needed.

How often does a rented home need one?

In England, private landlords need one at least every five years, or sooner if the report says so. Rules differ in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, so check the current guidance for where your property is.

Do I need to be there?

No. We arrange access with your tenant or agent and send everything to you.

Safe first, while you wait

  1. If it's safe to reach, switch off the main switch on your consumer unit (fuse board).
  2. Don't touch damaged sockets, switches, cables or anything that's wet.
  3. Keep everyone, and pets, away from the area.
  4. Call us on the number above and tell us what you can see, hear or smell.

Fire, smoke or someone has had a shock? Call 999 first. Don't touch a person who is still in contact with electricity.
