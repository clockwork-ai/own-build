On the day

What happens

  1. We check your consumer unit and earthing before anything else.
  2. We agree the exact spot for the charger with you, close to where you park.
  3. We run the cable as neatly as the house allows, inside where we can.
  4. We fit, set up and test the charger, then show you how it works.
  5. We sort the paperwork with the local network operator and leave you the certificate.

Common questions

EV charger installation: questions

What do I need for a home charger?

Usually off-street parking (a drive or garage) and a consumer unit with room for one more circuit. Send us a photo of your board and where you park and we can tell you most of what we need to know.

Can you fit a charger if I park on the street?

Generally no. Trailing a cable across the pavement isn't safe or allowed in most places. Some councils run on-street schemes, so it's worth asking yours.

Will I need a new fuse board?

Sometimes. If the board is old or full, it may need upgrading first. We'll tell you before you commit, and quote for both.

How long does it take?

Most installs are done in a day ${S}. Longer cable runs take a bit more.

Which charger should I choose?

We'll talk you through the options that suit your car, your parking and how you use it, and fit one you're happy with.

Want a clear quote?

Tell us about the job and send a photo if you can. We'll come back with a fixed quote, no surprises.

Safe first, while you wait

  1. If it's safe to reach, switch off the main switch on your consumer unit (fuse board).
  2. Don't touch damaged sockets, switches, cables or anything that's wet.
  3. Keep everyone, and pets, away from the area.
  4. Call us on the number above and tell us what you can see, hear or smell.

Fire, smoke or someone has had a shock? Call 999 first. Don't touch a person who is still in contact with electricity.
