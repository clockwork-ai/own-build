Common questions

Fault finding: questions

My fuse board keeps tripping. What should I do?

Switch off or unplug everything on that circuit, then reset the switch once. If it stays on, plug things back in one at a time to find the culprit. If it trips again with nothing connected, leave it off and call us.

Is flickering dangerous?

It can be harmless, like a failing bulb, or a sign of a loose connection. If it keeps happening, or you notice a smell or warmth, get it checked.

How long does fault finding take?

Many faults are found within an hour or two ${S}. Some intermittent ones take longer. We'll keep you posted as we go.

Safe first, while you wait

  1. If it's safe to reach, switch off the main switch on your consumer unit (fuse board).
  2. Don't touch damaged sockets, switches, cables or anything that's wet.
  3. Keep everyone, and pets, away from the area.
  4. Call us on the number above and tell us what you can see, hear or smell.

Fire, smoke or someone has had a shock? Call 999 first. Don't touch a person who is still in contact with electricity.
