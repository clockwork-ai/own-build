Five signs your fuse board is out of date

You don't need to open anything to spot an old board. Here's what to look for, and what to do about it.

Your consumer unit is the box that protects every circuit in your home. Many still in use are decades old. These are the signs we look for first.

1. Fuse wire instead of switches

If your board has pull-out fuses with fuse wire in them, it's an older design without the protection a modern board gives.

2. No RCD

Modern boards have RCDs, usually with a small test button marked T. They switch off fast if current leaks somewhere it shouldn't. No RCD means no protection of that kind.

3. A wooden box or back board

Older installations were often mounted on wood. Modern boards have metal enclosures.

4. Frequent tripping

Tripping is the board doing its job, but if it's happening often, something needs finding.

5. Warmth, buzzing or a burning smell

This one isn't about age. If your board is warm, buzzing or smells of burning, don't touch it. Switch off at the main switch if it's safe, and call an electrician.

Safe first, while you wait

  1. If it's safe to reach, switch off the main switch on your consumer unit (fuse board).
  2. Don't touch damaged sockets, switches, cables or anything that's wet.
  3. Keep everyone, and pets, away from the area.
  4. Call us on the number above and tell us what you can see, hear or smell.

Fire, smoke or someone has had a shock? Call 999 first. Don't touch a person who is still in contact with electricity.
