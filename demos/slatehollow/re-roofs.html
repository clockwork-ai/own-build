What we hear

Sound familiar?

  • Slates slipping all over the roof, not just in one place.
  • Years of patch repairs that keep failing.
  • A sagging ridge or a roof that looks wavy.
  • Damp in the loft in more than one spot.

On the day

What happens

  1. We arrange the scaffold and tell the neighbours.
  2. We strip one section at a time and check the timbers.
  3. New membrane and battens, then the slates or tiles.
  4. New ridge, valleys and lead flashing where it's needed.
  5. We clear the site every day and walk you round at the end.

Questions

Common questions

How long does a re-roof take?

A typical terrace takes one to two weeks ${S}, depending on the weather and the size.

Can you reuse my old slates?

Sometimes. Good natural slate can often be reused, mixed with matching new ones. We'll check before we quote.

Will my house be open to the rain?

No. We only strip what we can cover the same day, and we sheet it if the weather turns.

Do I need to be home?

Not all the time. We'll need access at the start and the end, and a way to reach the loft.

Safe first

  1. Don't go up a ladder or onto the roof. Not even to have a look.
  2. Inside, catch drips with buckets and move things out of the way.
  3. If water is near lights, sockets or the fuse board, switch off at the consumer unit, but only from somewhere dry.
  4. From the ground, take a few photos of the roof and any slates that have come down, then call us.

If anything's hanging loose over a path or the road, keep people well away from it and call us straight away.
