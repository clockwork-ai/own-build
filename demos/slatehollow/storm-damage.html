Storm damage

After the storm.

Stay safe, take photos from the ground, and let us do the climbing.

Send a photo quote

Do

  • Stay inside until the wind has dropped.
  • Keep people away from slates on paths and drives.
  • Take photos from the ground or a window: the whole roof, then any damage.
  • Catch any drips inside and move valuables.
  • Call a roofer and ask about a temporary cover if water's getting in.

Don't

  • Don't go up a ladder or onto the roof, not even to have a look.
  • Don't stand under anything that's hanging loose.
  • Don't touch wet lights or sockets. Switch off at the board from somewhere dry.

What we do

Make it safe, then make it right.

  1. We make it safe: loose slates down, a temporary cover over any hole.
  2. We look properly and photograph everything.
  3. We give you a clear quote for the permanent repair.
  4. If you're claiming on insurance, we can give you a written report and photos.

Safe first

  1. Don't go up a ladder or onto the roof. Not even to have a look.
  2. Inside, catch drips with buckets and move things out of the way.
  3. If water is near lights, sockets or the fuse board, switch off at the consumer unit, but only from somewhere dry.
  4. From the ground, take a few photos of the roof and any slates that have come down, then call us.

If anything's hanging loose over a path or the road, keep people well away from it and call us straight away.
