Flat roof options, in plain English

Felt, rubber or fibreglass: what each is like, and which suits which job.

Flat roofs aren't actually flat. They have a slight fall so the water runs off. The covering is what keeps it dry, and there are three you'll hear about most.

Felt

Layers of bitumen felt, the traditional choice. Fine for many garages and outbuildings.

Rubber (EPDM)

A single sheet with no joins in the middle. Good for extensions and dormers, and it copes well with our weather.

Fibreglass (GRP)

Laid as a liquid that sets hard. Neat finish, good for small roofs and where you might walk on it.

Which is best?

It depends on the size, the shape and how it's used. We'll look and explain the options, with a clear quote for each.

Safe first

  1. Don't go up a ladder or onto the roof. Not even to have a look.
  2. Inside, catch drips with buckets and move things out of the way.
  3. If water is near lights, sockets or the fuse board, switch off at the consumer unit, but only from somewhere dry.
  4. From the ground, take a few photos of the roof and any slates that have come down, then call us.

If anything's hanging loose over a path or the road, keep people well away from it and call us straight away.
