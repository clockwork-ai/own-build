What we hear

Sound familiar?

  • Gutters overflowing in heavy rain.
  • Drips from the joints.
  • Fascia boards that are soft, flaking or sagging.
  • Damp patches on the outside walls.

On the day

What happens

  1. We check the whole run, not just the leak.
  2. We remove the old gutter and any rotten boards.
  3. We fit new fascias and soffits where needed.
  4. We fit the new gutter with the right fall.
  5. We test it with water before we leave.

Questions

Common questions

Can you just clear my gutters?

Yes, we clear and check gutters. If they're damaged, we'll show you photos.

Why do my gutters overflow?

Usually a blockage, a sagging section or too little fall. Sometimes the gutter's just too small for the roof.

Do you need scaffolding?

Often not. For most houses we work from a tower or ladders, safely.

Send us a photo

Take a couple of photos from the ground and we'll tell you what we think, with a clear quote.

Safe first

  1. Don't go up a ladder or onto the roof. Not even to have a look.
  2. Inside, catch drips with buckets and move things out of the way.
  3. If water is near lights, sockets or the fuse board, switch off at the consumer unit, but only from somewhere dry.
  4. From the ground, take a few photos of the roof and any slates that have come down, then call us.

If anything's hanging loose over a path or the road, keep people well away from it and call us straight away.
