Services · Power flushing

Get the heat back into every radiator.

A power flush clears the sludge out of your heating system so the boiler works less and the house warms up faster.

Sound familiar?

What people ring us about

  • Radiators cold at the bottom, warm at the top.
  • Some rooms never get properly warm.
  • Brown or black water when you bleed a radiator.
  • A noisy boiler or pipes.
  • You're having a new boiler fitted and want the system clean first.

On the day

What happens

  1. We check the system and test how dirty the water is.
  2. We connect the flushing machine, usually where the pump is.
  3. We push clean water and cleaner through, radiator by radiator.
  4. We add inhibitor to protect the system from here on.
  5. We balance the radiators so they all heat evenly.

Questions

Power flushing: common questions

How long does a power flush take?

Most of a day for a typical home ${S}. Bigger systems take longer.

Will it fix my cold radiators?

If the cause is sludge, it usually makes a big difference. If it's a valve or the pump, we'll tell you.

Is it messy?

We protect the floors and work cleanly. Dirty water goes into our machine, not your carpets.

Do I need one before a new boiler?

A clean system helps a new boiler last. We'll check and advise.

While you wait

  1. Turn off the water at your stopcock. It's usually under the kitchen sink, or where the pipe comes into the house.
  2. If water is near sockets, lights or the fuse board, switch the electrics off at the consumer unit, but only if you can reach it from somewhere dry.
  3. Open the cold taps to drain the pipes, and catch drips with towels and buckets.
  4. Move anything valuable out of the way, then call us on the number above.

Smell gas? Don't use switches or flames. Open doors and windows, leave the property and call the National Gas Emergency number on 0800 111 999.
