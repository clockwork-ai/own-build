Six signs your radiators need a power flush

Cold patches, noisy pipes and slow heating. What they usually mean, and when a flush is worth it.

Over the years, the water in a heating system picks up rust and grit. It settles as sludge, usually at the bottom of radiators, and slowly makes the whole system work harder. These are the signs we look for.

1. Cold at the bottom, warm at the top

The classic sludge sign. Cold at the top is usually air, and bleeding sorts that.

2. Some radiators never get properly hot

Especially the ones furthest from the boiler.

3. Dark water when you bleed a radiator

Clear water is good. Brown or black water means there's muck in the system.

4. Noisy boiler or pipes

Kettling, banging or gurgling can point to sludge or a blockage.

5. Heating takes ages to warm up

If it used to be quicker, something's changed.

6. You're fitting a new boiler

A new boiler on a dirty system can struggle. Cleaning the system first protects it.

Is it always a flush?

No. Sometimes it's a valve, a pump or a single radiator. We check first and tell you straight.

General information only. Every home is different, so speak to a qualified engineer about yours. Never work on gas appliances yourself.

While you wait

  1. Turn off the water at your stopcock. It's usually under the kitchen sink, or where the pipe comes into the house.
  2. If water is near sockets, lights or the fuse board, switch the electrics off at the consumer unit, but only if you can reach it from somewhere dry.
  3. Open the cold taps to drain the pipes, and catch drips with towels and buckets.
  4. Move anything valuable out of the way, then call us on the number above.

Smell gas? Don't use switches or flames. Open doors and windows, leave the property and call the National Gas Emergency number on 0800 111 999.
