Emergency

Water where it shouldn't be?

Do these first, then call. Simple steps for the most common plumbing and heating emergencies.

Smell gas?

Don't use light switches, flames or anything that could spark. Open doors and windows, leave the property and call the National Gas Emergency number on 0800 111 999. Don't go back in until you're told it's safe.

Burst pipe

  1. Turn off the stopcock (usually under the kitchen sink).
  2. If water is near electrics, switch off at the fuse board, but only from a dry spot.
  3. Open the cold taps to drain the pipes.
  4. Catch what you can, move valuables, and call us.

No heating or hot water

  1. Check the boiler has power and the display is on.
  2. Check the pressure gauge. Most systems sit around 1 to 1.5 bar when cold.
  3. Check the thermostat and timer are calling for heat.
  4. Still nothing? Note any fault code and call us. Don't take the boiler's cover off.

A leak you can see

  1. If it's from a tap, toilet or appliance, use the small isolating valve on the pipe if there is one.
  2. Otherwise, turn off the stopcock.
  3. Put a bucket under it and towels round it.
  4. Take a photo and send it with your call-out request.

General safety information only. Never try to repair a gas appliance yourself.

While you wait

  1. Turn off the water at your stopcock. It's usually under the kitchen sink, or where the pipe comes into the house.
  2. If water is near sockets, lights or the fuse board, switch the electrics off at the consumer unit, but only if you can reach it from somewhere dry.
  3. Open the cold taps to drain the pipes, and catch drips with towels and buckets.
  4. Move anything valuable out of the way, then call us on the number above.

Smell gas? Don't use switches or flames. Open doors and windows, leave the property and call the National Gas Emergency number on 0800 111 999.
