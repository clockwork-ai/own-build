    While you wait

    1. Turn off the water at your stopcock. It's usually under the kitchen sink, or where the pipe comes into the house.
    2. If water is near sockets, lights or the fuse board, switch the electrics off at the consumer unit, but only if you can reach it from somewhere dry.
    3. Open the cold taps to drain the pipes, and catch drips with towels and buckets.
    4. Move anything valuable out of the way, then call us on the number above.

    Smell gas? Don't use switches or flames. Open doors and windows, leave the property and call the National Gas Emergency number on 0800 111 999.
