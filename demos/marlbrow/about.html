About

A small firm that designs gardens and builds them properly.

Marl is the heavy clay you hit a spade's depth down round here. A brow is the rise at the top of a field. Put them together and you've got most of West Lancashire, and most of what we dig into every day.

This is a fictional business. The story, the team and the numbers below are made up for this demo, and the pictures are illustrative or stock.

How it started Sample

Dan spent years on building sites digging footings and drains for other people. He kept seeing lovely new patios and drives laid on a few inches of nothing, sinking within a couple of winters. In 2014 Sample he bought a small digger and a plate compactor and started doing it the slow way.

A few years later Mo joined to draw the gardens and choose the planting, and the work changed. Now most jobs are whole gardens: the design, the hard landscaping, the planting and the drainage under it, built by the people who drew it.

Marlbrow is four people Sample, based in a yard in Ormskirk Sample. Mostly gardens for homes round West Lancashire, and a few small commercial jobs a year: a care home's grounds, a school's front, a shop's car park.

How we work

Four things we don't skip.

On a back garden or a care home car park, the same four rules.

  • We look and draw before we price

    Every job starts with a visit and a simple plan. Levels, water, access and what's under the ground all change the job, so we don't quote blind.

  • We check before we dig

    Pipes and cables aren't always where the plans say. We check for them before anything breaks ground.

  • We spend the time on the base

    Dig-out, sub-base and drainage are the parts you never see, and the parts that decide how long the garden lasts.

  • We leave it clean

    Road swept, pavement clear, skip gone. And we keep in touch once we've gone.
