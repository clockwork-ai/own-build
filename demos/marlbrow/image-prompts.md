# Marlbrow demo: illustrative image prompt sheet

For: whoever makes the illustrative photo sets for the Marlbrow Foundation demo (fictional garden design and landscaping firm, Ormskirk, West Lancashire).
Written: 11 November 2026. Site folder: `demos/marlbrow` on branch `demo/marlbrow-groundworks`.

There are **30 image slots**. The site already has a labelled placeholder in every one, so nothing breaks while they are missing. Save the finished files into the agreed Drive folder using exactly the paths below. They will be resized, compressed to WebP and pulled into the site.

## How to use this sheet

1. Each project starts with a **scene lock**. Paste the scene lock at the start of **every** prompt for that project, then add the shot prompt. That is what keeps the house, the garden, the light and the camera the same from one stage to the next.
2. **Before, during and finished share the same viewpoint.** Same camera position, height, lens and framing. Only the garden changes. Fixed things (house, trees, neighbours, sky and light) must not move or change shape. If you can, make the finished shot first and use it as the reference picture for before and during.
3. Detail shots can move in close, but they must clearly belong to the same job: same materials, same colours, same light.
4. Aspect ratios matter, because the before/after slider lays one picture exactly over the other. Before and finished for a project must be the **same aspect ratio and the same framing**.
5. Save as JPEG or PNG at the size given, or larger. They are converted to WebP on the way in.

## House style (applies to every prompt)

Add this to the end of every prompt:

> Photoreal photograph, natural colour, as if taken by a careful trade photographer on a full-frame camera. Ordinary West Lancashire suburban or village setting, UK. Soft daylight or light overcast unless stated. Realistic British materials and planting. No people, no animals, no text, no lettering, no signs with words, no logos, no watermarks, no readable number plates. Not CGI, not a render, not a show home, not a stately home, no tropical planting, no Mediterranean courtyard, no foreign street furniture. Slight natural imperfection is good: a few fallen leaves, real weathering on old brick.

If a shot really needs scale, one discreet figure seen from behind or at a distance is allowed. The default is nobody in frame.

## Project 1: A rear garden in Aughton (domestic, flagship)

Story on the site: a 1930s semi in Aughton. Tired lawn and broken slabs, turned into a porcelain patio with a step, a curved lawn, deep borders and low lighting.

### Scene lock: aughton-rear

> The rear garden of a 1930s semi-detached house in Aughton, West Lancashire. The house: two storeys, red-brown brick ground floor, off-white pebbledash render on the first floor, hipped roof in red clay tiles, white uPVC windows, white uPVC French doors in the centre of the ground floor, a black downpipe at the right-hand corner. The garden is flat, about 7 metres wide and 18 metres long. Both side boundaries are 1.8 metre timber fences. A tall silver birch in the neighbour's garden shows over the left-hand fence near the house. Viewpoint: camera at the far end of the garden, looking back up the garden towards the rear of the house, camera height 1.6 metres, 28 mm lens (full-frame equivalent), slightly off-centre to the left so the right-hand fence runs in from the right edge of the frame. The whole rear of the house is in frame with a strip of sky above the roof. Late May, mid-morning, light overcast, soft even light, dry.

### 6. before (`img/projects/aughton-rear/before.webp`, 3:2, 2400 x 1600)

> [Scene lock] Before the work. A patchy, mossy lawn with bare earth near the house. Next to the house, a small old patio of grey 600 mm concrete slabs, several cracked and sunken, weeds in the joints, one slab tilted. The right-hand fence is weathered orange-brown with one panel leaning. A rusty rotary washing line in the lawn. A narrow strip of tired, overgrown border with bare soil along the left fence. Nothing tidy, but not a rubbish tip: an ordinary neglected garden.

### 7. during (`img/projects/aughton-rear/during.webp`, 3:2, 2400 x 1600)

> [Scene lock] Mid-build, end of day, tidy site, nobody present. Old slabs and lawn removed. The patio area next to the house is dug out and filled with a flat, compacted grey crushed-stone sub-base. String lines on timber pegs mark the patio edge and the step, and a curved string line on pegs sets out the edge of the future lawn. The borders along both fences are dug over, dark soil, edges cut clean. New timber fence posts set along the right-hand side with the new panels not yet fitted. A plate compactor and a green wheelbarrow parked neatly to one side. A pallet of light grey porcelain slabs, wrapped, near the house. Same house, same birch, same light as the scene lock.

### 8. finished (`img/projects/aughton-rear/finished.webp`, 3:2, 2400 x 1600)

> [Scene lock] Finished. Across the full width of the house: a light grey porcelain patio, large 900 x 600 mm slabs in a stretcher bond, crisp narrow joints. One wide step, the full width of the patio, down to a gently curved lawn, freshly mown with stripes, crisp edges. Deep planted borders along both fences: ornamental grasses, lavender, white hydrangeas, purple alliums, catmint, and a small multi-stem amelanchier on the left. New close-board fence on the right, natural timber, straight and plumb. A narrow porcelain path runs along the left border towards the camera. Two small matt black outdoor wall lights beside the French doors (switched off). A simple outdoor table and two chairs on the patio. The house, birch, sky and viewpoint are exactly as in the scene lock.

### 9. dusk (`img/projects/aughton-rear/dusk.webp`, 3:2, 2400 x 1600)

> [Scene lock, but the time is late May at blue hour, about 30 minutes after sunset, clear deep-blue sky.] Exactly the finished garden from the same viewpoint. The two wall lights by the French doors are on, warm white. Small warm LED step lights glow in the riser of the patio step. A low uplighter lights the multi-stem amelanchier from below. Warm light from the kitchen through the French doors. Calm, understated lighting, not a light show. No visible cables.

### 10. detail-joint (`img/projects/aughton-rear/detail-joint.webp`, 4:3, 1600 x 1200)

> Close-up from the same Aughton job, same light grey porcelain patio and soft overcast light. Camera about 1 metre above the patio, looking down at 45 degrees. Even 4 mm joints filled flush with dark grey jointing compound. A square recessed drainage cover set into the patio, with the slabs cut neatly around it so the joint lines run straight through. Clean surface, a couple of fallen birch leaves for realism. Shallow depth of field.

### 11. detail-step (`img/projects/aughton-rear/detail-step.webp`, 4:3, 1600 x 1200)

> Close-up from the same Aughton job, same porcelain and same soft light. Low angle at the end of the patio step. The step tread is light grey porcelain with a crisp square edge, sitting on a riser faced in matching red-brown brick, neat flush pointing. A small square warm-white LED step light set into the riser (switched off). The edge of the lawn below the step, mown and neatly cut. Shows straight lines and a clean mitred corner.

### 12. detail-edge (`img/projects/aughton-rear/detail-edge.webp`, 4:3, 1600 x 1200)

> Close-up from the same Aughton job. Camera close to the ground at the point where the curved lawn meets the porcelain path along the border. A flush mowing edge of small dark grey granite setts follows the curve, bedded and pointed neatly, lawn mown right up to it with no gaps. Lavender and catmint spilling slightly over the far side. Same soft overcast light.

## Project 2: A front garden and drive in Ormskirk (domestic, one scheme)

Story on the site: a 1920s semi in Ormskirk. Cracked concrete drive and a scruffy front lawn, redesigned as one front garden: a block paved drive, a sandstone path, planting, a rebuilt wall and lighting, with drainage at the gate.

### Scene lock: ormskirk-front

> The front garden of a 1920s red-brick semi-detached house on a quiet, tree-lined residential road in Ormskirk, West Lancashire. The house: two storeys, red brick, a ground-floor bay window with a small red clay tiled canopy that continues over the front door, white timber-style sash windows, a front door painted deep green, a grey slate roof with a brick chimney. The plot is about 9 metres wide and 8 metres deep, with a low red-brick boundary wall to the pavement. A mature cherry tree stands in the neighbour's front garden on the right and overhangs slightly. Viewpoint: from the pavement edge opposite the left-hand side of the plot, camera height 1.6 metres, 35 mm lens (full-frame equivalent), looking slightly across the plot towards the house so the bay window, the front door and the whole front garden are in frame, with the pavement and the low wall along the bottom of the frame. Late September, mid-morning, light overcast, dry.

### 13. before (`img/projects/ormskirk-front/before.webp`, 3:2, 2400 x 1600)

> [Scene lock] Before. On the left, a cracked and oil-stained 1970s concrete drive, one car wide, with weeds in the cracks. On the right, a patchy front lawn with a narrow, cracked concrete path to the front door. The low front wall has partly collapsed near the gateway, with loose bricks on the ground. Two wheelie bins standing on the lawn. A few weeds along the base of the bay. Ordinary and tired, not derelict.

### 14. during (`img/projects/ormskirk-front/during.webp`, 3:2, 2400 x 1600)

> [Scene lock] Mid-build, tidy site, nobody present, no vehicles on the plot. The old concrete and lawn are gone. The drive area is dug out and filled with a flat, compacted grey crushed-stone sub-base. New grey concrete edging kerbs are set on concrete haunching along the line of a new curved planting bed. A channel drain with a metal grating is laid across the drive entrance at the pavement. The front wall has been rebuilt to its full low height in matching red brick, with fresh mortar. A covered pile of sharp sand on a board, and a neat stack of wrapped block paving on a pallet. String lines mark the path to the front door.

### 15. finished (`img/projects/ormskirk-front/finished.webp`, 3:2, 2400 x 1600)

> [Scene lock] Finished front garden designed as one scheme. On the left, a block paved drive, one car wide, in tumbled charcoal and silver-grey blocks laid herringbone, with a darker single border course, finishing at a flush channel drain across the entrance. A curved planted bed between the drive and the path, with ornamental grasses, lavender, a small Japanese maple and low evergreen shrubs, dressed with dark bark. A buff sandstone path from the pavement to the front door with one sandstone step. The low front wall, rebuilt in matching red brick with a brick-on-edge coping, and two square brick pillars at the path entrance. A slatted timber bin store, screened by planting, at the side. Two low matt black bollard lights along the path (switched off). No car on the drive. Same house, same cherry tree, same viewpoint and light as the scene lock.

### 16. detail-drain (`img/projects/ormskirk-front/detail-drain.webp`, 4:3, 1600 x 1200)

> Close-up from the same Ormskirk job, same charcoal and silver-grey herringbone block paving and same overcast light. Camera about 1 metre up, looking down at the drive entrance. A slim channel drain with a galvanised steel grating set perfectly flush with the blocks, the blocks cut cleanly and tightly up to it, the paving falling very slightly towards the drain. Kiln-dried sand in the joints. A couple of cherry leaves for realism.

### 17. detail-kerb (`img/projects/ormskirk-front/detail-kerb.webp`, 4:3, 1600 x 1200)

> Close-up from the same Ormskirk job. Where the herringbone block paving meets its darker border course and the edge of the planted bed. Cut blocks along the herringbone edge are tight and straight, joints even, border course in a straight unbroken line, bark-mulched bed and grasses beyond. Low angle, soft overcast light.

### 18. detail-wall (`img/projects/ormskirk-front/detail-wall.webp`, 4:3, 1600 x 1200)

> Close-up from the same Ormskirk job. The rebuilt low front wall in red brick matching the house, with a brick-on-edge coping. Neat, consistent flush pointing in a light mortar, straight courses, crisp end at the brick pillar. Lavender in the bed behind the wall. Side-on angle along the wall to show it is dead straight.

## Project 3: A garden room in Burscough (domestic, structure)

Story on the site: a 1990s detached house in Burscough. An overgrown corner with a rotten shed, turned into a garden room on a level base, with a paved apron, a stepping-stone path across the lawn, new fencing and planting.

### Scene lock: burscough-room

> The bottom of the long back garden of a 1990s detached house in Burscough, West Lancashire. Looking towards the back right-hand corner of the garden. Fixed elements: a mature apple tree on the right third of the frame; the gable end of the neighbour's red-brick house visible over the left-hand fence in the distance; along the back boundary, flat farmland and a hawthorn hedge beyond the fence line. Viewpoint: standing on the lawn about 10 metres from the back boundary, camera height 1.6 metres, 35 mm lens (full-frame equivalent), facing the back corner, apple tree on the right. Early June, midday, bright overcast, dry.

### 19. before (`img/projects/burscough-room/before.webp`, 3:2, 2400 x 1600)

> [Scene lock] Before. The back corner is overgrown with brambles and nettles. A small rotting timber shed with a cracked window and a sagging felt roof sits in the corner. The ground is uneven, the lawn rough and long. The back fence is old, grey and leaning in places. The apple tree is unpruned.

### 20. during (`img/projects/burscough-room/during.webp`, 3:2, 2400 x 1600)

> [Scene lock] Mid-build, tidy site, nobody present. Shed and brambles gone, the corner cleared and levelled. A new rectangular concrete base, about 4 by 4.5 metres, freshly cast inside straight timber formwork boards held by pegs, smooth and level, a black damp-proof membrane edge visible neatly at one side. String lines on pegs mark a path across the lawn. A spirit level lying on the base. New concrete fence posts set along the back boundary. A neat stack of sandstone slabs on a pallet. The apple tree has been pruned.

### 21. finished (`img/projects/burscough-room/finished.webp`, 3:2, 2400 x 1600)

> [Scene lock] Finished. In the back corner stands a single-storey timber garden room about 4 metres wide, clad in vertical charcoal-stained larch boards, flat roof with a slim dark edge, large glazed sliding doors in the front with the interior just visible (a desk and chair). In front of it a buff sandstone paved apron about 1 metre deep, the full width of the room. A path of sandstone stepping stones runs across the lawn towards the camera. Along the back boundary, a new close-board timber fence on concrete posts with concrete gravel boards. Planting around the room: ferns, hydrangeas, grasses and a climbing hydrangea starting on the fence. The lawn is mown and the apple tree is pruned and healthy. Same viewpoint, same tree, same neighbour's gable and same light as the scene lock.

### 22. detail-base (`img/projects/burscough-room/detail-base.webp`, 4:3, 1600 x 1200)

> Close-up from the same Burscough job, during the build, same bright overcast light. A long aluminium spirit level resting on the smooth new concrete base, the bubble dead centre. The crisp straight edge of the base and the timber formwork just beyond it, with the black membrane lapped neatly at the edge. Shallow depth of field, the apple tree soft in the background.

### 23. detail-path (`img/projects/burscough-room/detail-path.webp`, 4:3, 1600 x 1200)

> Close-up from the same Burscough job. Buff sandstone stepping stones set flush into the lawn, evenly spaced at a natural stride, the turf cut cleanly around each one with no gaps or lips. Camera low and looking along the line of stones towards the garden room, which is soft in the background.

### 24. detail-fence (`img/projects/burscough-room/detail-fence.webp`, 4:3, 1600 x 1200)

> Close-up from the same Burscough job. The new boundary fence: a slotted concrete post, a concrete gravel board at the bottom, close-board timber above with a capping rail, everything plumb and straight. A fern and hydrangea at its foot, the mown lawn edge in front. Side-on angle along the fence line to show it is straight.

## Project 4: Care home grounds and visitor parking in Rufford (light commercial)

Story on the site: a small care home in Rufford. A worn car park, a stepped path and tired beds, turned into an eight-bay visitor car park with an accessible bay, a gently sloping block paved path to the door, raised planters at seat height and new planting. Keep it modest and domestic in scale. It must not look like a civil-engineering job.

### Scene lock: rufford-care

> The front grounds of a small two-storey care home in Rufford, West Lancashire: a converted Victorian red-brick house with a newer single-storey red-brick wing on the right, white windows, grey slate roofs, and a white-framed glazed entrance porch in the centre. A mature copper beech tree stands on the left of the grounds. Viewpoint: from the visitor car park entrance at the front left of the site, camera height 1.7 metres, 28 mm lens (full-frame equivalent), looking across the car park and front garden towards the entrance porch, the beech tree on the left edge. Mid-April, mid-morning, soft overcast light, dry. No signs, no people, no cars.

### 25. before (`img/projects/rufford-care/before.webp`, 3:2, 2400 x 1600)

> [Scene lock] Before. A worn, patched tarmac car park with potholes, a puddle and faded, barely visible bay lines. A narrow cracked concrete path from the car park to the porch with a single awkward step up. Overgrown shrubs against the house, a tired lawn with bare patches, a broken timber bench. Ordinary wear, not derelict.

### 26. during (`img/projects/rufford-care/during.webp`, 3:2, 2400 x 1600)

> [Scene lock] Mid-build, tidy and safe, nobody present. The car park area is dug out and filled with compacted grey crushed-stone sub-base, with new concrete kerbs set along its edges. A new wide path towards the porch is being formed, with timber shuttering showing a gentle slope instead of a step. Low brick walls for raised planters half built in red brick. Neat grey temporary mesh fence panels on feet around the work area, without any branding. A covered pile of sand and a pallet of buff blocks.

### 27. finished (`img/projects/rufford-care/finished.webp`, 3:2, 2400 x 1600)

> [Scene lock] Finished. A new dark tarmac visitor car park with eight bays marked in crisp white lines, including one wider accessible bay with a hatched access zone painted in white (no symbols or text). Granite-coloured kerbs with a dropped kerb to the path. A wide, gently sloping path to the entrance porch, in buff block paving with a contrasting charcoal edge course, no step anywhere. Two raised planters at seat height in red brick with smooth buff stone coping, planted with lavender, rosemary and grasses, with simple timber benches beside them. A neat, mown lawn and low planting against the house. Three low matt black bollard lights along the path. No cars. Same building, beech tree, viewpoint and light as the scene lock.
