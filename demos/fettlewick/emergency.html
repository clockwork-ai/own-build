Small jobs that cannot wait

Some things will not keep until next week

A door that will not shut, a handrail come loose, a gate down where the dog gets out. Ring for those rather than filling a form in. There is no round the clock line on this demo and no response time printed anywhere, because neither would be true.

Ring us about these

  • A door or window that will not shut or lock, so the house cannot be left
  • A handrail, banister or grab rail that has come away from the wall
  • A gate or fence panel down where a dog or a child can get out
  • A shelf, a television bracket or a heavy mirror pulling off the wall
  • A floorboard, step or threshold somebody is catching a foot on
  • A shed or outbuilding that will not lock

These are all jobs one person with a van can make safe quickly, and all jobs that get worse or more expensive if they are left.

Ring somebody else about these

  • Anything on the heating system, which is a heating engineer
  • Water coming through a ceiling or a pipe that has burst, which is a plumber
  • A socket, switch or light that is tripping, buzzing or scorched, which is an electrician
  • Anything on a roof or needing a tall ladder, which is a roofer
  • A crack that has appeared and is moving, which is a builder or an engineer
  • Being locked out of your own house, which is a locksmith

We will say this on the phone rather than take the booking and leave you waiting. If you are not sure which of those it is, ring and describe it and we will tell you.

If you can smell burning, or there is water near anything electrical

Turn the power off at the consumer unit if it is safe to get to, and ring an electrician. If anybody is in danger, ring the emergency services first. None of that is a job for a handyman and we would not pretend it was.

The most common urgent one, and why it happens

A door that will not shut is the call we get most. It is almost never the door and almost always the hinge side, which is good news, because it means it can usually be made to shut again in minutes.

If it will not shut at all, ring. If it just needs a nudge, it can go on an ordinary list. Door and lock adjustments

What to expect

What happens when you ring

You describe it, we ask two or three questions, and then one of three things happens. We come out, we book you in for the next sensible slot, or we tell you plainly that it is a job for another trade and which one.

We will not give you a time we cannot keep. If something is already running over we will ring and say so rather than let you sit in waiting for a van.

Making it safe is not the same as finishing it

Sometimes the right answer on the day is to make something safe and come back properly with the right part. A handrail screwed back into something solid today and re fixed properly next week is better than a handrail left hanging for a fortnight.

Is there an out of hours line?

No, and this demo will not pretend there is. Sample hours are Mon to Fri 8am to 5.30pm, Sat 9am to 12.

What if I am not sure who I need?

Ring and describe it. Working out which trade you want is free and takes a minute.

Do you charge more for an urgent visit?

Can you just make it safe for now?

Often, yes. We will tell you what has been made safe and what still needs doing properly.
