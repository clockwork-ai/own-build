About

A made up firm, built carefully

Fettlewick Property Repairs does not exist. It was invented so a real handyman could look at a finished site rather than a mock up, and everything on it was written and made for that.

The idea behind the firm

Fettlewick is a northern word dressed up: to fettle something is to put it in order. The firm is one person and a van, working across Sefton, taking lists rather than single jobs, and being straight about where the line is.

That last part is the whole character of it. A handyman who will have a go at anything is worth less than one who tells you on the phone that what you have is a job for an electrician. This site is written as though that is the pitch, because for this trade it is.

What is real on this site

The trade knowledge is real. Doors drop on the hinge side. Dot and dab leaves a gap behind the board. Timber rots in a band at the ground line. Flat pack goes out of square before the back goes on. All of that is general trade knowledge and it is the same on your house as on an invented one.

The firm, the people, the reviews, the jobs and the towns on the job cards are all invented. The pictures were generated for this demo. There is no address, no company number, no accreditation and no years in business, because inventing any of those would be the one thing that makes a demo dishonest.

The numbers on this site

01632 960614 is a demo number from the range Ofcom keeps aside for television and film, so it rings nobody. The email address ends in .example, which is reserved and cannot receive anything. The only real number anywhere on this site is 07460 038202, in the bar at the top and the note at the bottom, and that is Clockwork's number rather than the firm's.

What is not here on purpose

No prices. No response times. No star ratings pulled off somewhere. No logos of schemes nobody has joined. No photograph of a van with a name on it. A real firm fills those in with things that are true, and this one cannot, so it leaves them out rather than making them up.

How it works

The same five stages, whoever you are

Send the list

Write down the room, the thing and what is wrong with it. Photographs help and nothing has to be in order.

We go through it

We say what we would get on with, what needs a part ordering and what we would rather you gave to another trade.

The visit

We arrive with the list, work down it, and tell you if something turns out bigger than it looked on paper.

Ticked off

Everything done gets ticked on your list, and anything we left is written down with the reason.

What is left

If there is a job for somebody else, you get a plain note of what it needs so you can ring the right trade.

The services, the work and the advice are all written out in full.

The ones worth ringing about

  • A door or a window that will not shut or lock, so the house cannot be left
  • A handrail, a banister or a grab rail come loose on the stairs
  • A gate or a fence panel down where a dog or a child can get out
  • Something heavy on a wall that has started to pull away from it
  • A floorboard, a step or a threshold strip somebody is catching a foot on

These need a different trade, today

Anything on the heating system, water coming through a ceiling, or a socket or a light that has started tripping or scorching. Ring a heating engineer, a plumber or an electrician for those. We would rather say so on the phone than take the job and leave you waiting.
