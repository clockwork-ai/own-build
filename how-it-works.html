    Two kinds of help.

    Most "automation" you've heard of is the first kind. What I build is mostly the second.

    Normal automation

    A row of dominoes.

    When this happens, do that, the same way every time. For example: when someone fills in your contact form, send them an email. It's useful, but it can't cope with anything unexpected.

    What I build

    A capable office assistant.

    You give it a goal, not a script, and it works out the steps. It reads what comes in, makes sensible decisions, uses your tools (email, calendar, WhatsApp, your job software) and only comes to you when it really needs you.

    You stay in control.

    It works for you, not instead of you. You decide how much it does on its own.

    Your rules
    What it can say, what it can book, and what it must always check with you, like prices, discounts or anything unusual.
    Your wording
    Nothing goes out in your name unless you've approved the wording.
    Every step logged
    Every message and booking is logged, so you can check it any time.
    Your tools
    It works with what you already use where possible: your phone, WhatsApp, email, calendar and job software.
    When the website is yours
    From day one if you pay upfront, or after your 12th monthly payment. When it's yours, you get a full copy of the files and code, or I move it into your own account. If you stop the monthly payments before the 12th, the site comes offline. The domain name is in your name either way. Hosting and care is a separate monthly service you can cancel any month. The AI systems run on a monthly plan. I don't set a minimum term on this page. The detail goes in writing before you start.
