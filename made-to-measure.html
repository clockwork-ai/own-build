    Who it's for.

    Most trades are well served by Foundation, Growth or Autopilot. This is for the ones whose paperwork has a shape of its own.

    More than one site
    Several branches, depots or vans working to different diaries, and one office trying to keep up.
    Odd workflows
    Surveys with their own report, stage sign-offs, compliance paperwork, or a way of pricing nobody else does.
    Customers who need their own view
    Landlords, agents or tenants who want to see where a job is up to without ringing you.
    Heavier reporting
    Monthly figures for a board, a partner or a big customer, in the format they ask for.
    Your own tools
    A checklist, a calculator or a form your team uses every day that lives on paper or in a spreadsheet nobody else understands.
    Step one

    Blueprint week.

    Before anything is built, we spend a week on paper. You come out of it with a plan you own, whether you go ahead or not.

    1. How your jobs really run

      We sit with you and whoever does the office side, and follow a job from the first call to the last invoice. Where it slows down, where it gets typed twice, where things go missing.

    2. The screens

      Rough drawings of every screen you and your team would use, on a phone first. You can point at them and say "no, it goes like this".

    3. The information

      What gets recorded on each job, who needs to see it, and what has to be kept for how long.

    4. What we reuse

      A plain list of which parts we already run and can reuse: logins for you and your team, texts and emails that go out with your yes, every enquiry in one place, and the monthly report. Then what's new and has to be built.

    The Blueprint is paid for, and what you pay comes off the build if you go ahead. You get the price before the week starts.

    Step two

    The build.

    A fixed price from the Blueprint, agreed before we start. No day rates and no "it took longer than we thought".

    Yours

    Built for your business, on its own.

    Your build has its own plan and is kept separate from everyone else's. Your information stays yours.

    Not from scratch

    The parts we already run, reused.

    We don't rebuild what already works. Review requests after every job, messages that only go out with your yes, enquiries in one place and the monthly report all come across as they are. You pay for what's new to you, not for us to reinvent the rest.

    You check it

    A private preview before it goes live.

    You and your team try it on your own phones, tell us what's wrong, and it goes live when you're happy.

    Step three

    Monthly care.

    Once it's live, we look after it every month, so it keeps working while you get on with the jobs.

    Small changes
    A new field on a form, a wording change, a new job type. Ask, and it's done.
    Kept running
    Hosting, backups and updates looked after, and somebody watching that it's up.
    The everyday parts
    Review requests, approved messages, your enquiries and the monthly report keep running as part of care.
    A real person
    You ring or message Callum. Not a ticket queue.

    Care is a monthly plan, sized to the build. You get the figure in writing with the build price.
