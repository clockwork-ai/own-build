    Privacy, in plain English.

    What we collect, why, who else sees it, and what you can ask us to do. Last updated 22 October 2026.

    12:00Privacy

    Who we are.

    Clockwork AI is run by Callum Broadfoot in Manchester. We decide how your details are used, so we're responsible for them.

    Questions about your data: email callum@clockworkai.app or ring 07985 379605.

    What we collect.

    When you book
    Your name, email, phone number and anything you write in the notes. Bookings are made through Cal.com and go into Callum's Google Calendar.
    When you get in touch
    Your number or email address, and what you tell us, when you call, text, WhatsApp or email.
    When you visit
    Our host, Vercel, keeps standard server logs, such as your IP address and browser, to run and protect the site. Our fonts load from Google Fonts, which also sees your IP address.
    Cookies
    We don't use analytics or advertising cookies. The calendar on our booking page comes from Cal.com, which may set its own.

    Why we use it.

    To reply to you, hold the call you booked, and give you the quote or the Clockwork Check you asked for. The law calls this taking steps you've asked for before a contract, and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries.

    We never sell your details, and we don't add you to marketing lists.

    Who else sees it.

    Only the services we need to run the business.

    Cal.com
    Takes your booking.
    Google
    Calendar, email inbox and the fonts on this site.
    ImprovMX
    Forwards email sent to clockworkai.app.
    Vercel
    Hosts this website.
    WhatsApp
    Only if you message us there.

    Some of these companies store data outside the UK, under the safeguards UK law requires.

    How long we keep it.

    If an enquiry doesn't turn into work, we delete your details within 12 months.

    If you become a client, we keep your details while we work together, and our business records for as long as the law requires for tax.

    When we build for you.

    The systems we build run in your tools and handle your customers' details. Those details are yours. We only use them to set things up, fix them and look after them, on your instructions, and we put that in writing before we start.

    Your rights.

    You can ask us for a copy of your details, ask us to correct or delete them, or object to how we use them. Email callum@clockworkai.app and we'll reply within a month.

    If you're not happy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
