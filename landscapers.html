    Engine running, gloves on. The customer rings the next firm.

    A third of micro businesses failed to answer incoming calls, and 69% of callers who reached voicemail hung up without leaving a message (Moneypenny, 2016).

    On a digger, laying a patio, or in a garden with the mixer going, you can't answer. Most garden enquiries come in the evening and at weekends, when people are looking out at the back and planning. By Monday they've booked someone else's visit.

    The other jobs pile up around it. The design quote from last week sits in your notes. The family who love their new garden never get asked for a Google review, and the best photos stay on your phone. Last spring's maintenance customers don't hear from you until they ring someone else.

    Six parts, built for a landscaping firm.

    1. Missed calls texted back

      Texts back missed and out-of-hours calls. It asks what the garden needs, gets the photos and books the site visit, including while you're on the digger. It is upfront that it isn't you.

    2. A chat box on your site

      Website enquiries, with the photos and the postcode, land in one list. It answers "do you do porcelain patios in Ormskirk?" in your words.

    3. Database Reactivation

      Wins back old quotes and past customers. Polite follow-ups on quotes that have gone quiet, stopped the moment they reply.

    4. Review Accelerator

      Reviews and aftercare. Once a job is marked done, texts go out in your name. Same day: a thank-you and a Google review link, for every customer. About 3 days later: one nudge if there's no review. About 2 weeks: "Everything still OK with the job?" A problem comes straight to you, as well as the link, never instead of it. At 2 and 6 months: "How's things going?" At 12 months: a check-up or service reminder.

    5. Referral Flywheel

      A simple ask, once the job is done, to send someone else your way. No reward scheme invented on this page. The later check-ins in the Review Accelerator keep you in mind first.

    6. Lifetime Value System

      Maintenance plans and seasonal check-ins. A tidy-up before spring. A hedge and lawn visit at the end of summer. The 12-month check-up text is part of the Review Accelerator.
